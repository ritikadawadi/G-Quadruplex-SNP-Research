Main interpretation
- On average, TSS mutant sequences show a decrease in maximum g-score relative to wild type.
- Many TSS SNPs do not change the top-level summary values, but they still often change motif sequence composition.
- The balance of effect classes suggests that weakening or preserving QGRS potential is more common than strengthening it in this region.
