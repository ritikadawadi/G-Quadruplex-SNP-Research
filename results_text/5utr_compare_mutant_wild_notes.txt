5'UTR Wild vs Mutant QGRS Comparison Notes

Location of comparison outputs
- compare_mutant_wild/5utr/5utr.comparison_summary.tsv
- compare_mutant_wild/5utr/5utr.motif_deltas.tsv
- compare_mutant_wild/5utr/5utr.overall_summary.tsv

What was compared
- The original wild-type 5'UTR QGRS results were compared against the mutant 5'UTR QGRS results.
- Wild-type inputs came from:
  - initial_fasta/results_5utr/5utr_reference.qgrs_summary.tsv
  - initial_fasta/results_5utr/5utr_reference.qgrs_hits.tsv
- Mutant inputs came from:
  - mutant_qgrs/5utr/5utr_mutant.qgrs_summary.tsv
  - mutant_qgrs/5utr/5utr_mutant.qgrs_hits.tsv
- The mutant manifest was used to connect each mutant sequence back to the exact SNP:
  - mutant_fasta/5utr/5utr.mutant_manifest.tsv

How the comparison was done
- Each mutant 5'UTR sequence was compared only to its matching original wild-type 5'UTR sequence.
- For each SNP, the comparison tracked:
  - reference QGRS count
  - mutant QGRS count
  - change in QGRS count
  - reference maximum g-score
  - mutant maximum g-score
  - change in maximum g-score
  - reference maximum tetrad count
  - mutant maximum tetrad count
  - whether the top-scoring QGRS sequence changed
  - how many motif instances were gained
  - how many motif instances were lost
- A motif-level comparison was also done by comparing the motif sequence, g-score, tetrad count, and motif length between wild-type and mutant outputs.

Overall 5'UTR comparison summary
- Total mutant comparisons: 406
- Mean delta QGRS count: -0.113
- Mean delta max g-score: -3.377
- SNPs with higher max g-score: 18
- SNPs with lower max g-score: 126
- SNPs with unchanged max g-score: 262
- SNPs with more QGRS motifs: 2
- SNPs with fewer QGRS motifs: 48
- SNPs with unchanged QGRS count: 356
- SNPs with at least one gained motif instance: 359
- SNPs with at least one lost motif instance: 405
- SNPs where the top motif sequence changed: 264

Interpretation of the summary
- The 5'UTR set shows a stronger average negative effect than the TSS set.
- On average, G-related SNPs in 5'UTR QGRS regions slightly reduced motif count and reduced the maximum g-score more noticeably than in TSS.
- Many SNPs changed the top motif sequence even when the summary-level counts or scores stayed the same.
- This means 5'UTR SNPs often alter motif composition without always changing the headline statistics.

Effect classes observed
- no_summary_change: 244
- lower_max_gscore: 95
- qgrs_lost_all: 30
- higher_max_gscore: 18
- fewer_qgrs_same_max_gscore: 17
- fewer_qgrs_lower_max_gscore: 1
- more_qgrs_same_max_gscore: 1

What the effect classes mean
- no_summary_change:
  - the mutant had the same total QGRS count and same max g-score as the wild type
  - but the underlying motif sequence may still have changed
- lower_max_gscore:
  - the strongest mutant motif scored lower than the strongest wild-type motif
- qgrs_lost_all:
  - all detectable QGRS motifs in that mutant sequence disappeared
- higher_max_gscore:
  - the mutant created a stronger top-scoring motif than the wild type
- fewer_qgrs_same_max_gscore:
  - the number of motifs decreased, but the best score stayed the same
- fewer_qgrs_lower_max_gscore:
  - the number of motifs decreased and the strongest score also dropped
- more_qgrs_same_max_gscore:
  - additional motifs appeared, but the best score stayed the same

How to read the output files

comparison_summary.tsv
- One row per mutant SNP sequence.
- Best for answering:
  - Did the SNP increase or decrease the max g-score?
  - Did the SNP increase or decrease the motif count?
  - Did the top motif sequence change?
  - Which SNPs had the strongest effects?

motif_deltas.tsv
- One row per motif gain or motif loss event.
- Best for answering:
  - Which exact QGRS motifs were lost?
  - Which exact QGRS motifs were gained?
  - Did the mutant create new motifs or remove existing motifs?

overall_summary.tsv
- One summary row for the whole 5'UTR comparison dataset.
- Best for high-level reporting on a poster or in notes.

Main biological takeaways from the 5'UTR comparison
- G-related SNPs in 5'UTR QGRS regions frequently altered motif composition.
- The average effect was slightly negative for both motif number and maximum g-score.
- Loss of G often weakened the best motif or removed motifs completely.
- A smaller but important subset of to_G mutations strengthened the best motif.
- Many 5'UTR SNPs changed the exact top motif sequence even when count and max g-score stayed unchanged.

Bottom line
- The 5'UTR comparison step is complete.
- The results show, per SNP, how the mutant 5'UTR sequence differs from the wild type in QGRS burden and g-score.
- The next comparable step is to summarize the 3'UTR comparison in the same way and then combine all three regions.
