TSS Wild vs Mutant QGRS Comparison Notes

Location of comparison outputs
- compare_mutant_wild/tss/tss_upstream500.comparison_summary.tsv
- compare_mutant_wild/tss/tss_upstream500.motif_deltas.tsv
- compare_mutant_wild/tss/tss_upstream500.overall_summary.tsv

What was compared
- The original wild-type TSS QGRS results were compared against the mutant TSS QGRS results.
- Wild-type inputs came from:
  - initial_fasta/results_tss/tss_upstream500_reference.qgrs_summary.tsv
  - initial_fasta/results_tss/tss_upstream500_reference.qgrs_hits.tsv
- Mutant inputs came from:
  - mutant_qgrs/tss/tss_upstream500_mutant.qgrs_summary.tsv
  - mutant_qgrs/tss/tss_upstream500_mutant.qgrs_hits.tsv
- The mutant manifest was used to connect each mutant sequence back to the exact SNP:
  - mutant_fasta/tss/tss_upstream500.mutant_manifest.tsv

How the comparison was done
- Each mutant TSS sequence was compared only to its matching original wild-type TSS sequence.
- For each SNP, the comparison tracked:
  - reference QGRS count
  - mutant QGRS count
  - change in QGRS count
  - reference maximum g-score
  - mutant maximum g-score
  - change in maximum g-score
  - reference maximum tetrad count
  - mutant maximum tetrad count
  - whether the top-scoring QGRS sequence changed
  - how many motif instances were gained
  - how many motif instances were lost
- A motif-level comparison was also done by comparing the motif sequence, g-score, tetrad count, and motif length between wild-type and mutant outputs.
- This means the analysis was not limited to only summary-level changes. It also checked which exact motifs were gained or lost.

Overall TSS comparison summary
- Total mutant comparisons: 307
- Mean delta QGRS count: -0.081
- Mean delta max g-score: -1.41
- SNPs with higher max g-score: 4
- SNPs with lower max g-score: 58
- SNPs with unchanged max g-score: 245
- SNPs with more QGRS motifs: 1
- SNPs with fewer QGRS motifs: 26
- SNPs with unchanged QGRS count: 280
- SNPs with at least one gained motif instance: 281
- SNPs with at least one lost motif instance: 307
- SNPs where the top motif sequence changed: 113

Interpretation of the summary
- Most SNPs did not change the summary-level maximum g-score.
- However, many SNPs still changed the actual motif composition even when the summary statistics stayed the same.
- This means a SNP can alter which G4/QGRS motif is present without necessarily changing the maximum score or total motif count.
- On average, the effect of the TSS G-related SNPs slightly reduced QGRS burden and slightly lowered the maximum g-score.

Effect classes observed
- no_summary_change: 218
- lower_max_gscore: 58
- fewer_qgrs_same_max_gscore: 26
- higher_max_gscore: 4
- more_qgrs_same_max_gscore: 1

What the effect classes mean
- no_summary_change:
  - the mutant had the same total QGRS count and same max g-score as the wild type
  - but the underlying motif sequence may still have changed
- lower_max_gscore:
  - the strongest mutant motif scored lower than the strongest wild-type motif
- fewer_qgrs_same_max_gscore:
  - the total number of motifs decreased, but the best motif score stayed the same
- higher_max_gscore:
  - the mutant created a stronger top-scoring motif than the wild type
- more_qgrs_same_max_gscore:
  - the total number of motifs increased, but the top score stayed the same

How to read the output files

comparison_summary.tsv
- One row per mutant SNP sequence.
- Best for answering:
  - Did the SNP increase or decrease the max g-score?
  - Did the SNP increase or decrease the motif count?
  - Did the top motif sequence change?
  - Which SNPs had the strongest effects?

motif_deltas.tsv
- One row per motif gain or motif loss event.
- Best for answering:
  - Which exact QGRS motifs were lost?
  - Which exact QGRS motifs were gained?
  - Did the mutant create new motifs or remove existing motifs?

overall_summary.tsv
- One summary row for the whole TSS comparison dataset.
- Best for high-level reporting on a poster or in notes.

Main biological takeaways from the TSS comparison
- G-related SNPs in TSS QGRS regions can change G4 potential in several ways:
  - reduce the maximum g-score
  - increase the maximum g-score
  - reduce the total number of motifs
  - change the identity of the top motif without changing the summary numbers
- Most SNPs did not change the summary-level max g-score, but many still altered motif composition.
- Loss of G most often reduced or preserved G4/QGRS potential rather than improving it.
- A smaller number of to_G changes strengthened the top-scoring motif.

Bottom line
- The TSS comparison step is complete.
- The results now show, per SNP, how the mutant sequence differs from the wild type in QGRS burden and g-score.
- The next comparable step would be to run the same wild-vs-mutant comparison for 5'UTR and 3'UTR and then summarize all three regions together.
