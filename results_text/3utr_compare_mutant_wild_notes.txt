3'UTR Wild vs Mutant QGRS Comparison Notes

Location of comparison outputs
- compare_mutant_wild/3utr/3utr.comparison_summary.tsv
- compare_mutant_wild/3utr/3utr.motif_deltas.tsv
- compare_mutant_wild/3utr/3utr.overall_summary.tsv

What was compared
- The original wild-type 3'UTR QGRS results were compared against the mutant 3'UTR QGRS results.
- Wild-type inputs came from:
  - initial_fasta/results_3utr/3utr_reference.qgrs_summary.tsv
  - initial_fasta/results_3utr/3utr_reference.qgrs_hits.tsv
- Mutant inputs came from:
  - mutant_qgrs/3utr/3utr_mutant.qgrs_summary.tsv
  - mutant_qgrs/3utr/3utr_mutant.qgrs_hits.tsv
- The mutant manifest was used to connect each mutant sequence back to the exact SNP:
  - mutant_fasta/3utr/3utr.mutant_manifest.tsv

How the comparison was done
- Each mutant 3'UTR sequence was compared only to its matching original wild-type 3'UTR sequence.
- For each SNP, the comparison tracked:
  - reference QGRS count
  - mutant QGRS count
  - change in QGRS count
  - reference maximum g-score
  - mutant maximum g-score
  - change in maximum g-score
  - reference maximum tetrad count
  - mutant maximum tetrad count
  - whether the top-scoring QGRS sequence changed
  - how many motif instances were gained
  - how many motif instances were lost
- A motif-level comparison was also done by comparing the motif sequence, g-score, tetrad count, and motif length between wild-type and mutant outputs.

Overall 3'UTR comparison summary
- Total mutant comparisons: 638
- Mean delta QGRS count: -0.133
- Mean delta max g-score: -0.417
- SNPs with higher max g-score: 12
- SNPs with lower max g-score: 42
- SNPs with unchanged max g-score: 584
- SNPs with more QGRS motifs: 3
- SNPs with fewer QGRS motifs: 88
- SNPs with unchanged QGRS count: 547
- SNPs with at least one gained motif instance: 563
- SNPs with at least one lost motif instance: 638
- SNPs where the top motif sequence changed: 118

Interpretation of the summary
- The 3'UTR set shows a milder average effect on max g-score than the 5'UTR set.
- Most 3'UTR SNPs left the summary-level maximum g-score unchanged.
- Even so, many SNPs still changed motif composition or reduced motif count.
- This means 3'UTR SNPs often preserve the top summary value while still reshaping the underlying QGRS landscape.

Effect classes observed
- no_summary_change: 496
- fewer_qgrs_same_max_gscore: 85
- lower_max_gscore: 39
- higher_max_gscore: 12
- more_qgrs_same_max_gscore: 3
- fewer_qgrs_lower_max_gscore: 2
- qgrs_lost_all: 1

What the effect classes mean
- no_summary_change:
  - the mutant had the same total QGRS count and same max g-score as the wild type
  - the motif sequence may still have changed
- fewer_qgrs_same_max_gscore:
  - the total number of motifs decreased, but the strongest motif kept the same score
- lower_max_gscore:
  - the strongest mutant motif scored lower than the strongest wild-type motif
- higher_max_gscore:
  - the mutant created a stronger top-scoring motif than the wild type
- more_qgrs_same_max_gscore:
  - the total number of motifs increased, but the best score stayed the same
- fewer_qgrs_lower_max_gscore:
  - the motif count decreased and the strongest motif also weakened
- qgrs_lost_all:
  - all detectable QGRS motifs in that mutant sequence disappeared

How to read the output files

comparison_summary.tsv
- One row per mutant SNP sequence.
- Best for answering:
  - Did the SNP increase or decrease the max g-score?
  - Did the SNP increase or decrease the motif count?
  - Did the top motif sequence change?
  - Which SNPs had the strongest effects?

motif_deltas.tsv
- One row per motif gain or motif loss event.
- Best for answering:
  - Which exact QGRS motifs were lost?
  - Which exact QGRS motifs were gained?
  - Did the mutant create new motifs or remove existing motifs?

overall_summary.tsv
- One summary row for the whole 3'UTR comparison dataset.
- Best for high-level reporting on a poster or in notes.

Main biological takeaways from the 3'UTR comparison
- Most 3'UTR SNPs did not change the summary-level max g-score.
- However, many still reduced total motif number or altered motif identity.
- Loss of G was more commonly associated with unchanged or weaker QGRS behavior than with improvement.
- A smaller subset of to_G mutations increased the top motif strength.
- Compared with 5'UTR, the average g-score effect in 3'UTR was milder, but motif turnover was still common.

Bottom line
- The 3'UTR comparison step is complete.
- The results now show, per SNP, how the mutant 3'UTR sequence differs from the wild type in QGRS burden and g-score.
- With TSS, 5'UTR, and 3'UTR all compared, the project is now ready for a combined cross-region interpretation step.
