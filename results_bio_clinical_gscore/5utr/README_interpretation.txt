5′UTR — how to read these results

Clinical significance (ClinVar CLNSIG) and COSMIC role in cancer annotate genes and variants;
they are not direct measurements of G-quadruplex biology.

• pct_gscore_changed: fraction in that bucket with any change in QGRS max g-score.
• stacked figure: green = changed max g-score; grey = unchanged.
• mean_abs_delta_when_changed: mean |mutant−wild| among rows with change only.

Caveats: many non-coding ClinVar entries are VUS or benign; pathogenic bins may be small.
COSMIC roles are comma-exploded — one variant can count in more than one role.
Association only — not causality.
